Privacy Policy

Set Ready · Effective October 24, 2026

Set Ready ("the app") is made by Neil Valdez for the Set Ready Foundation. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, or the identifier Apple provides if you use Sign in with Apple. Signing in is optional; without an account your data stays on your device.
  • Name you choose: the display name you set is shown to other members on the leaderboard and in the shot gallery.
  • Your progress: set days you log (date, hours, department, steps you enter), modules passed, and the gear in your toolbox. If you sign in, this syncs to our database and is visible to other signed-in members as part of the leaderboard. You can hide yourself from the leaderboard in Account.
  • Shots you upload: photos and video clips you submit to Master the Shot, with your caption. They are visible to other signed-in members, along with the ratings they receive.
  • Ratings, reports and blocks: the stars you give, content you report, and members you block.
  • Crew directory profile (optional): if you list yourself, the name, department, positions, city, union local, bio, links, availability, toolbox and badges you choose are shown to anyone, including on the public web directory, so producers can find and hire you. Your email and phone are shown to signed-in Set Ready members, and on the public web directory only if you turn that on. You can edit, unlist or remove your profile at any time in the app.
  • Set Link (optional): if you start or join a set, your name and department are shown to the other crew on that set, along with the messages and reports (camera logs, sound reports, script notes) you share there. Only members of that set can see them. Messages are deleted automatically after 14 days, and you can delete your own messages and reports at any time. Set Link never sends notifications.
  • Call sheets on Set Link (optional): call sheet details and any call sheet PDF or photo you share to a set are stored privately and shown only to that set's members.
  • Location (optional): only when you tap a location button in the Mileage tracker, to measure how far a set is from the home base you saved. Locations and your mileage log stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Kept only on your device: calorie estimates and body weight, camera logs, sound reports, script notes, call sheets, your resume and IMDb credit tracker. These never leave your phone unless you share or export them yourself.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't read your phone's health data.

Service providers

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. Apple provides Sign in with Apple. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can delete your account, synced progress, uploaded shots and ratings at any time in the app: Account → Delete my account. You can delete individual shots from the shot's page.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules let each member change only their own data.

Contact

Questions or requests: setreadyfoundation.org/contact. We respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
