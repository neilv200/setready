Delete your Set Ready account

Set Ready by the Set Ready Foundation

In the app

Open Set Ready, go to Account, and tap Delete my account. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email info@setreadyfoundation.org from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Set Ready account". If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us the name on your account so we can find it. We'll delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile and crew directory listing, synced progress and set days, your answers to the first-run questions, training credit requests and the files attached to them, shots and photos you uploaded, ratings, Set Link messages, voice clips, reports, receipts and paperwork you sent to a production office, and continuity photos you shared.

What we keep

Nothing tied to you. Totals used for grant reports (for example, how many members chose each department) don't include names or emails.
