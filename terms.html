Community rules

Set Ready is a professional training community. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't upload or post:

  • Nudity, sexual content, or anything involving minors in an unsafe way
  • Violence, gore, weapons misuse, or dangerous stunts presented as instruction
  • Hate, harassment, threats or bullying
  • Footage of people who didn't agree to be filmed, or anything shot on a production where you weren't allowed to share it (NDAs apply)
  • Work that isn't yours
  • In Set Link: photos are not supported, and you must not post script pages, plot or cast details, or anything else your production's NDA covers
  • False information in a crew directory profile, including claiming credits, union membership, training or skills you don't have, or listing someone else

Every shot can be reported, and you can block any member. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts that post it are suspended or deleted.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you upload. You give the Set Ready Foundation permission to store it and show it to other members in the app. You can delete it at any time.

Estimates and training

Calorie estimates, pay and overtime estimates, lens and depth-of-field calculations, and turnaround times are approximate. Your contract, deal memo and equipment manuals decide the real rules. Safety information in the app is awareness training and does not replace OSHA courses or your production's safety instructions.
